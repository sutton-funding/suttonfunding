Editorial approach

How these guides are prepared

Guides are general educational content authored by Henry Gross, CEO, and reviewed under Sutton Funding's editorial policy. They use primary government and regulator sources where available, distinguish general product descriptions from specific offers and link readers to the controlling source material.

See how to report a possible factual error in the corrections policy.

Important limitation

A guide is not an offer

These pages do not state Sutton Funding eligibility, rates or guaranteed outcomes. Product availability and terms vary by applicant and provider. A binding agreement exists only when the business and applicable provider execute final documents.
