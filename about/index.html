About Sutton Funding

Sutton Funding is the trade name of 8CH GEE VENTURES INC, a New York-based small business funding company. Sutton Funding is not a bank.

What Sutton Funding does

Sutton Funding helps small and mid-sized businesses explore working capital, equipment financing, lines of credit and related business-purpose products. Options may be available through Sutton Funding programs and through a network of third-party funders, lenders and partners.

Specific products, providers, terms, pricing and availability depend on the applicant, underwriting, verification and final approval. Sutton Funding does not promise that an application will result in an offer or funding.

How applications are handled

The company collects information about applicants and their businesses to evaluate requests, verify information, identify potential funding options, present prospective terms and support the application and account process. Application information may be shared with prospective providers and service providers as described in the Privacy Policy.

A calculator result, pre-qualification message or prospective term sheet is not a binding offer. A funding agreement becomes binding only when the business and the applicable provider execute final written closing documents.

Funding information without hidden assumptions

Business owners should know who the funding provider is, how much the business will receive, the total repayment obligation, the payment schedule, every fee and any guarantees or security interests before signing. Sutton Funding's funding disclosures summarize these points and link to the controlling legal policies.
