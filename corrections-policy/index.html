Accuracy and transparency

Sutton Funding corrections policy

How to report a possible error in educational content and how a correction is reviewed and reflected on the page.

Effective: August 9, 2026Last updated: August 9, 2026

Report a possible error

Email info@suttonfunding.com with the subject “Content correction.” Include the page URL, the passage you believe is inaccurate, why it may be incorrect and any primary source that supports the report.

How reports are reviewed

The passage is checked against the cited source, the controlling government or regulator material where applicable, and Sutton Funding's published disclosures or policies when the statement concerns the company. A disagreement about a general conclusion is distinguished from a verifiable factual error.

How corrections appear

When a factual error is confirmed, the page is corrected and its update date is revised. A substantial correction may also be described on the page when that context helps readers understand what changed. Minor spelling, grammar, formatting or link repairs may be made without a separate correction note.

No fixed response deadline

Review time depends on the issue and the sources needed to verify it. This policy does not promise a fixed response or publication deadline.

Editorial standards

For the standards applied when guides are prepared and updated, read the editorial policy. For general company or privacy questions, use the contacts on the contact page.
